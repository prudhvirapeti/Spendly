Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only the information necessary to provide the Service:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data — basic logs such as login timestamps, used solely for security and debugging.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or device identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to operate Spendly for you:

  • Displaying your expenses and spending summaries.
  • Authenticating your identity when you sign in.
  • Sending essential account emails (e.g. password reset) if implemented.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond providing the Service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon deletion, your data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties. We may use minimal infrastructure services (e.g. hosting providers) that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements. These providers are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Export your expense data at any time.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out via the contact information provided in the app. We aim to respond to all privacy-related requests within 7 days.
